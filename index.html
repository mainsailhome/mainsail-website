    Home/Senior Services/Aging in Place

    Stay safe and independent in the home you love

    We help seniors and their families find the risks and fix them, with patience and respect.

    Most seniors want to stay in their own homes as long as they can. The biggest threat to that is usually a fall, and most falls happen in the bathroom, on stairs or on the way to the bathroom at night.

    The good news is that the fixes are usually simple, affordable and done in a day or two. A sturdy grab bar, a second handrail and better lighting can make a home dramatically safer without making it look like a hospital.

    Common aging-in-place upgrades

    • Grab bars in tubs and showers
    • Stair and entry handrails
    • Lever door handles
    • Brighter lights and night lights
    • Non-slip floors and tubs
    • Shower and raised toilet seats
    • Removing trip hazards
    • Lower shelves and closet rods
    • Battery smoke and CO detectors

    Our aging-in-place process

    1. Free walk-through

      We walk through the home together and note loose rugs, dark stairs, slick tubs and hard-to-turn knobs.

    2. Written plan with prices

      You get a simple list of fixes, most important first, with a price for each. No pressure.

    3. Installed right

      Grab bars go into studs or proper anchors, rails are solid, and we clean up before we leave.

    4. Ongoing check-ins

      Add a maintenance plan and we come back each season to check batteries, filters and the small stuff.

    Home/Senior Services/Safety Walk-Through

    Free home safety walk-through

    About an hour, room by room, with a written list of fixes and prices when we're done.

    A fresh set of eyes catches things you stop noticing when you live with them every day: the rug that curls, the dim stairway, the tub with nothing to hold onto.

    We look at the home the way a person with weaker balance, eyesight or grip would move through it, and we point out what would help most.

    What happens during the visit

    1. We talk first

      What's getting harder? Stairs, the shower, getting up at night? We start with what matters to you.

    2. Room-by-room check

      Bathroom, bedroom, stairs, kitchen, entry and outside, using our safety checklist.

    3. Written plan

      A list of fixes, most important first, with a price for each. Keep it even if you hire someone else.

    4. You decide

      Do one fix, all of them, or none. There's no pressure and no charge for the visit.

    Adult children are welcome to join by phone or video, or we can send them the written plan and photos.

    Home/Senior Services/Safety Checklist

    Room-by-room safety checklist

    Use this list to check a parent's home yourself, or call and we'll walk it with you for free.

    Bathroom

    • Grab bars by the toilet and in the tub or shower
    • Non-slip surface in the tub
    • Shower seat
    • Raised toilet seat
    • Night light on the path from the bed

    Stairs & halls

    • Solid handrails on both sides
    • Light switches at top and bottom
    • No loose carpet or worn treads
    • Contrast tape on step edges
    • Clear walkways with no cords

    Bedroom

    • Lamp or switch reachable from bed
    • Clear path to the bathroom
    • Motion night lights
    • Smoke and CO detector nearby
    • Closet rods and shelves within reach

    Kitchen

    • Everyday items on lower shelves
    • Bright light over counters and stove
    • Easy-grip cabinet pulls
    • Working smoke detector
    • Non-slip mats or no rugs

    Entry & doors

    • Lever handles instead of knobs
    • Railing at porch and entry steps
    • Low or no threshold to trip on
    • Bright porch light
    • Key lockbox for caregivers

    Outside

    • Even walkways with no lifted pavers
    • Clean, non-slick steps and decks
    • Working motion lights
    • Railings tight and secure
    • House number visible for responders

    Home/Water & Air Quality

    What's really in your water and air?

    Free in-home water & air quality checks

    Hard water leaves spots on dishes, scale on fixtures and film on your skin. Humidity, dust and pollen affect the air you breathe at home. Free in-home checks show where you stand and what will help.

    What better water does for you

    • Softer skin and hair
    • Spot-free dishes and glasses
    • Less soap scum and scale
    • Brighter, longer-lasting clothes
    • Protects pipes and appliances
    • Use less soap and detergent

    How the free water test works

    1. We test at your sink

      About 30 minutes, right in your kitchen.

    2. We explain the results

      Hardness, chlorine and other common issues, in plain English.

    3. You see your options

      If treatment makes sense, we show which system fits your home. No obligation.

    Water softeners

    Remove the hard minerals that cause spots, scale and dry skin.

    Drinking water

    Reverse osmosis at the kitchen tap for clean, great-tasting water.

    Whole-house filtration

    Carbon filtration for every faucet and shower in the home.

    Every system is sized to your water, your home and your household, and backed by the manufacturer's warranty.

    • Better humidity control in a humid climate

    Home/FAQs

    Frequently asked questions

    How fast can you come out?

    Most estimates go out within 24 hours. Small repairs can often be scheduled the same week.

    Is the home safety walk-through really free?

    Yes. We walk through the home, point out risks and leave a written list with prices. You decide what, if anything, to fix.

    Do you work with property management companies?

    Yes. We handle turnovers, work orders and punch lists, and send before-and-after photos with every invoice.

    What does the water test involve?

    We test your tap water right at the sink in about 30 minutes, explain the results and show you the options. There's no obligation.

    Is there a minimum charge?

    Yes, a small minimum covers the trip for very short jobs. Bundle a few small items into one visit to get the most out of it.

    Are you a licensed contractor? Do you do electrical and plumbing?

    No. Mainsail is a handyman, painting and home-update service, not a licensed contractor, so we don't do electrical, plumbing or permitted work. If your project needs a licensed electrician, plumber or contractor, we'll tell you up front and refer you to licensed pros we trust.

    Do you buy the materials?

    We can pick up everything for the job, or use materials you already have. Materials are listed separately on your quote.

    Can you help after a storm?

    Yes. After a storm we help with fence and gate repair, debris haul-off, screen and soffit repairs and getting your home back in shape.

    How do I pay?

    You'll get an invoice by text or email after the job, and you can pay by card or bank transfer.

    Can family members set up service for a parent?

    Absolutely. We'll keep you updated by text with photos after each visit.
